Operating model

Built for accreditation, not retrofitted.

ISO 17025 / 15189 by design

Our own laboratories run, when operating, the ISO 17025 and ISO 15189 operating model we build for clients. Accreditation is held against a defined scope of tests and applies while those tests are in operation. The methodology is not theoretical.

In-house research

Dedicated laboratories that generate scientific data and evidence, rather than coordinating other people’s. The scientist who designs the method is the scientist who runs it.

Senior accountability

A named scientific lead connected directly to project quality and delivery, written into the engagement rather than offered informally.
